# Ethics and Professional Practice

- The Dashboard is an educational research artifact, not financial advice.
- Historical classification and backtest results are not promises of future
  profitability.
- AAPL and NVDA are separately fitted replications and are not presented as
  independent proof for all equities.
- Formal results are displayed from reviewed prediction and decision ledgers;
  exploratory Page 7 outputs are clearly separated.
- Data timing, next-open execution, purged temporal boundaries, and fixed cost
  assumptions are stated explicitly.
- Literature is used to motivate constructs and methods. Exact parameter
  settings are labelled as project choices rather than universal optima.
- Internal checks demonstrate technical consistency, not future predictive
  validity.
